Aquanact Engine Documentation Roadmap
=====================================

Book Alignment Goal
-------------------

Confirm the engine still follows the architecture ideas emphasized in Jason Gregory's
Game Engine Architecture: clear runtime architecture, distinct tools and asset
pipeline, separated subsystems, explicit update flow, and practical engine
boundaries.
